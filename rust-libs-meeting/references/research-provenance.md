## Generator and bot behavior

- Inspected local `../libs-team` at clean commit `a42791873701a0ed2c926c729bed078f1c351b23`: [generator.rs](https://github.com/rust-lang/libs-team/blob/a42791873701a0ed2c926c729bed078f1c351b23/tools/agenda-generator/src/generator.rs), [cli.rs](https://github.com/rust-lang/libs-team/blob/a42791873701a0ed2c926c729bed078f1c351b23/tools/agenda-generator/src/cli.rs). `libs_agenda`, `write_fcps`, `GithubQuery::write`, `dedup`, and `github_api` establish the selection, sorting, deduplication and pagination caveats in the evidence guide.
- Fetched [rfcbot's public API](https://rfcbot.rs/api/all) and GitHub's paginated issue-comment API. The API exposed `fcp.fk_initiating_comment`, `fk_bot_tracking_comment`, `fcp_start`, `fcp_closed`, reviewer pairs, active concerns, issue data, and tracking-comment text. Use those identifiers to inspect the actual proposal rather than reconstructing authority from labels.
- [`final fn` coordination proposal](https://github.com/rust-lang/rust/issues/160394#issuecomment-5188873903) explicitly selects `lang,types` despite `T-libs`; [its status comment](https://github.com/rust-lang/rust/issues/160394#issuecomment-5188875192) retained a struck-through resolved concern while the live API returned zero active concerns. The [`repr(C)` ZST proposal](https://github.com/rust-lang/rust/pull/157973#issuecomment-4984493401) selects `lang,opsem`. These establish why label-only ownership and text-search concern counts mislead.
- [`Command` debug proposal](https://github.com/rust-lang/rust/pull/132484#issuecomment-2472310685) had all six reviewers checked but two active concerns at retrieval. All checkboxes completed is not readiness.
- The [RawWakerVTable status receipt](https://github.com/rust-lang/rust/pull/158186#issuecomment-4760293866) uses a majority threshold with at most two approvals outstanding, and [its start receipt](https://github.com/rust-lang/rust/pull/158186#issuecomment-5496046130) records the actual transition. Do not interpret the generator's total unchecked count as the number needed to enter FCP.
- The [rfcbot README](https://github.com/rust-lang/rfcbot-rs#final-comment-period) documents explicit team selection, concern commands, and cancellation. At research time its all-reviews wording lagged live status receipts. The [std-dev-guide meeting page](https://std-dev-guide.rust-lang.org/team/meetings.html) still described separate meetings and older nomination labels. Current generator and proposal evidence take precedence over those stale operational details.
